Privacy Policy for All-In Poker Manager

Effective Date: March 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to All-In Poker Manager. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by All-In Poker Manager and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

All-In Poker Manager is primarily a utility application used to track home poker games. To facilitate multiplayer synchronization (Poker Groups), we utilize Google Firebase as our backend service. The information we process includes:

  • Player Names: The display names you enter for players in your games.
  • Game Data: Records of buy-ins, cash-outs, and chip counts related to the games you log.
  • Device Information: Basic analytics and crash reporting information collected via Firebase to help improve the app's stability.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app, specifically:

  • To sync live poker game data across devices participating in the same Poker Group.
  • To maintain a history of your past poker games.
  • To identify and fix app crashes and performance issues.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (a suite of services provided by Google) for data storage, synchronization, and crash reporting. Firebase may collect certain device information as outlined in their privacy policy. You can review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your game data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or cellular networks is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

All-In Poker Manager is not directed to individuals under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by creating an issue on our GitHub repository.
