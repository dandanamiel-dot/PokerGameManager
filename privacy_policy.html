Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

All-In Poker Manager ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Player names — entered by you to identify participants in your poker games. These are stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
  • Game data — buy-in amounts, cash-out values, and session results. Stored locally on your device.
  • Group information — if you create or join a poker group, the group name, currency preference, and member list are stored in Firebase Firestore.

b) Automatically collected

  • Anonymous user identifier (UID) — Firebase Authentication generates an anonymous UID to associate your device with your poker groups. No email, phone number, or name is collected.
  • Crash and performance data — Firebase may collect anonymous diagnostic data to improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To enable poker group syncing across devices (Firebase Firestore)
  • To maintain your game history and player statistics locally on-device
  • To allow you to share settlement results with other players (only when you explicitly tap "Share")

3. Data Storage

  • Local data — Player names, game sessions, buy-ins, and cash-outs are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is not transmitted to any server unless you join a group.
  • Cloud data — Poker group membership and group settings are stored in Google Firebase Firestore (US-based). Firebase is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Settlement summaries are only shared when you explicitly tap the "Share Settlement" or "Share Summary" button, using your device's native share sheet.

5. Data Retention

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete the app.
  • Group data in Firebase is retained until the group is deleted by its owner.
  • You can delete all local data by deleting and reinstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

All-In Poker Manager is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 17.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, Firestore database

8. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your group data at any time by contacting us or by using the "Delete Group" option in the app settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
